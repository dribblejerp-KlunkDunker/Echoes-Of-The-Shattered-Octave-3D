Port Cantabile, the Long Mole

A proof scene for the harbour: the Long Mole in granite flags, with iron bollards, rope swags and coils, a timber jetty and moored boats (each shroud with its chime), the iron mast-tower and the derrick with the Mole-bell at the Mole-head, the Harbour Office and the stone harbour houses, water that reflects them, and across the harbour the cliff with the Salt Stair's lamps climbing to the counting-house.
