Oakhaven, the square

A proof scene for the new look: the square rebuilt in real 3D, with scanned stone, plaster, slate and cobbles, an overcast autumn sky, wet stones, lantern light, the iron-oak, the Chapel with its empty belfry, the Boar & Dulcimer's sign, and the Consortium's mooring mast over the quarry to the north.
